Core Hypothesis: Training spatial attention maps to align with clinical bounding boxes (explanation supervision) produces explanations that are measurably more faithful than post-hoc Grad-CAM — while preserving competitive classification AUC at zero extra inference cost. Tested on all three backbones (ResNet-50, DenseNet-121, EfficientNet-B0) across 14 thoracic pathologies.

Model Architecture

Explanation-Supervised Attention CNN
CNN Backbone

ImageNet-pretrained feature extractor. Outputs 7×7 feature map. Three variants: ResNet-50, DenseNet-121, EfficientNet-B0.

Spatial Attention (★ core)

1×1 conv → sigmoid → 7×7 map A. Feature-weighted: F' = F ⊙ A. Trained to align with bounding boxes via L_attn.

Channel Attention

CBAM-style squeeze-excitation. Optional; improves classification without spatial penalty.

L_cls — Classification

Focal loss + class-weighted BCE. Handles severe label imbalance across 14 classes.

L_attn — Explanation Supervision

BCE between upsampled attention map and bounding-box mask. Primary driver of IoU improvement (+34.7%).

L_corr — Co-occurrence

Penalises clinically implausible label combinations using a co-occurrence matrix. Helps AUC without spatial benefit.

Key Findings

Statistically Significant (DeLong p < 3×10⁻³¹)

📍 Localisation Gain

+34.7%

Relative IoU improvement for ResNet50-Attn vs post-hoc Grad-CAM on the same backbone. DenseNet121-Attn achieves best absolute IoU at 0.3178.

⚖️ Classification Trade-off

−9.9%

AUC cost for ResNet50-Attn vs baseline (0.7603 vs 0.8434). Expected: attention supervision shifts capacity toward spatial alignment. L_corr partially recovers classification.

🎯 Loss Ablation

L_attn

Primary driver of IoU gain. L_corr contributes classification recovery without spatial benefit. Together they form the best trade-off operating point.
